Indian Law Master Revision Manual

Criminal Law, Constitutional Safeguards, Torts, Contracts, and Intellectual Property Rights
1. Constitutional Law Safeguards & Immunities
Part III Protections in Penal Matters:
  • Article 20 1: Protection against ex post facto penal laws; no retrospective criminal liability or enhanced penalty.
  • Article 20 2: Protection against double jeopardy; no individual can be prosecuted and punished twice for the same offense.
  • Article 20 3: Right against self-incrimination; an accused cannot be compelled to give testimony against themselves.
Sovereign & Legislative Immunities:
  • Article 105 2 & Article 194 2: Complete immunity for Members of Parliament and State Legislative Assemblies for speech or votes inside legislatures.
  • Article 361 2 & 3: Absolute immunity for the President and Governors; no criminal proceedings or arrest warrants during office.
2. General Exceptions IPC Ch IV & BNS Ch III
Universal Defenses Declaring Conduct as NO OFFENSE:
  • Mistake of Fact IPC 76 & 79 / BNS 14 & 17: Acts done under good-faith mistake of fact believing oneself bound or justified by law. Mistake of law is not excused.
  • Judicial Acts & Orders IPC 77 & 78 / BNS 15 & 16: Acts of judges within bona fide jurisdiction; ministerial execution of warrants.
  • Accident IPC 80 / BNS 18: Lawful act done with lawful means and reasonable care without criminal intent.
  • Necessity IPC 81 / BNS 19: Acts done without criminal intention to avoid greater imminent harm.
  • Infancy IPC 82 & 83 / BNS 20 & 21: Absolute immunity under 7 years doli incapax; qualified immunity between 7 and 12 years if immature.
  • Insanity IPC 84 / BNS 22: Unsoundness of mind destroying capacity to know the nature or wrongfulness of the act McNaughten Rule.
  • Involuntary Intoxication IPC 85 / BNS 23: Intoxicating substance administered without knowledge or against will.
  • Private Defense IPC 96–106 / BNS 34–44: Justifiable force for defending body or property. Extends to causing death IPC 100/103 & BNS 38/41 against threats of death, grievous hurt, rape, robbery, or night house-breaking.
3. Substantive Penal Offenses IPC & BNS
A. Criminal Negligence: IPC Sec 304A / BNS Sec 106 1; Hit-and-run BNS Sec 106 2.
Punishment: IPC 304A up to 2 years or fine; BNS 106 1 up to 5 years and fine doctors up to 2 years; Hit-and-run up to 10 years and fine.
When NOT Punishable: Pure unavoidable accidents with due care; professional errors without gross negligence.
B. Culpable Homicide & Murder: Homicide IPC 299/304 & BNS 100/105; Murder IPC 300/302 & BNS 101/103.
Punishment: Culpable Homicide: Life or up to 10 years and fine. Murder: Death or Life Imprisonment, and fine.
When NOT Murder: Grave and sudden provocation, bona fide excess of self-defense, sudden fight, or adult consent. Not an offense if within lawful self-defense.
C. Wrongful Restraint & Confinement: Restraint IPC 339/341 & BNS 126; Confinement IPC 340/342 & BNS 127.
Punishment: Restraint up to 1 month or ₹500 fine; Confinement up to 1 year or ₹1000 fine up to 3 years if exceeding 10 days.
When NOT Punishable: Good-faith obstruction of private way over land/water; lawful police arrest.
D. Sexual Offenses Rape: IPC Sec 375/376 & BNS Sec 63/64; Gang rape IPC 376D / BNS 70 1.
Punishment: Standard: Rigorous imprisonment not less than 10 years up to natural life; Gang rape: not less than 20 years up to life; Minor under 12: 20 years to life or death.
When NOT Punishable: Consensual acts between two adults aged 18 or above; legitimate surgical care.
E. Property Crimes Theft, Extortion, Robbery, Dacoity: Theft IPC 378/379 & BNS 303; Extortion IPC 383/384 & BNS 308; Robbery IPC 390/392 & BNS 309; Dacoity IPC 391/395 & BNS 310.
Punishment: Theft/Extortion: Up to 3 years or fine; Robbery: Rigorous imprisonment up to 10 years up to 14 on highway at night; Dacoity: Life or rigorous imprisonment up to 10 years.
When NOT Punishable: Bona fide claim of legal ownership; lawful attachment under court warrant.
F. Commercial & White-Collar Offenses: Misappropriation IPC 403 / BNS 314; Breach of Trust IPC 405/406 & BNS 316; Cheating IPC 415/420 & BNS 318.
Punishment: Misappropriation up to 2 years; Breach of Trust up to 3 years up to 5 under BNS; Cheating with delivery of property Sec 420 up to 7 years and fine.
When NOT Punishable: Pure civil breach of contract without initial fraudulent intent; bonafide lost-property safekeeping.
4. Law of Torts Civil Wrongs
Foundational Maxims: Injuria Sine Damno legal wrong without damage is actionable Ashby v White; Damnum Sine Injuria damage without legal wrong is not actionable Gloucester Grammar School.
Universal Tort Defenses When NOT Liable: Volenti non fit injuria consent, Act of God vis major, inevitable accident, private defense, statutory authority.
Core Liability Doctrines:
  • Strict Liability Rylands v Fletcher: Non-natural use of land and escape of dangerous thing. Defenses: act of God, plaintiff fault, third party act.
  • Absolute Liability MC Mehta v UOI: Inherently hazardous or dangerous activity; strict liability with no defenses or exceptions.
  • Vicarious Liability: Master liable for torts of servant committed in the course of employment.
5. Law of Contracts Indian Contract Act 1872
Valid Contract Essentials Sec 10: Offer, acceptance, lawful consideration, capacity, free consent, lawful object.
Agreements Void Ab Initio No Legal Effect:
  • Minor Agreement Sec 11: Completely void from the start Mohori Bibee v Dharmodas Ghose.
  • Unlawful Object/Consideration Sec 23: Forbidden by law or contrary to public policy.
  • Restraint of Trade Sec 27 & Legal Proceedings Sec 28: Void to that extent.
  • Wagering Agreements Sec 30: Betting agreements are void.
  • Doctrine of Frustration Sec 56: Performance becoming impossible or unlawful post-formation without fault.
Voidable Contracts Vitiated Consent: Coercion Sec 15, Undue Influence Sec 16, Fraud Sec 17, Misrepresentation Sec 18.
6. Intellectual Property Rights IPR
Trade Marks Act 1999: Protects distinctive brand names, logos, and marks. Term: 10 years renewable indefinitely. Infringement punishment: Imprisonment 6 months to 3 years, and fine ₹50000 to ₹200000. Not infringement: Non-commercial use, honest concurrent use.
Copyright Act 1957: Protects original literary, dramatic, musical, and artistic works. Term: Author life plus 60 years. Infringement punishment: Imprisonment 6 months to 3 years, and fine ₹50000 to ₹200000. Not infringement Sec 52: Fair dealing for research, private study, review, or reporting.
Patents Act 1970: Protects new, non-obvious industrial inventions. Term: 20 years non-renewable. Remedy: Civil injunctions and damages. Not infringement: Experimental research or government compulsory licensing.
